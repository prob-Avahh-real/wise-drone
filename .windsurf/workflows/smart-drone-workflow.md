## Smart Drone Workflow

The smart drone workflow involves three main phases:

1. **Monitoring**: The drone continuously monitors its surroundings using sensors and cameras.
2. **Decision Making**: The drone analyzes the data from the sensors and cameras to determine the best course of action.
3. **Action**: The drone takes the determined action, such as flying to a specific location, taking photos, or performing a specific task.

During each phase, the drone can communicate with a remote control or a central server to receive instructions or send data. The key components of the smart drone are:

- **Sensors**: Sensors such as GPS, altimeter, accelerometers, and cameras provide the drone with information about its surroundings.
- **Computer Vision**: Advanced computer vision algorithms analyze the images captured by the cameras to identify objects, detect patterns, or recognize specific conditions.
- **Decision Making**: The drone uses machine learning algorithms or heuristics to process the data from the sensors and cameras and make decisions based on the context and goals.
- **Actuators**: The drone's actuators, such as motors or servos, control the movement of the drone and perform specific actions based on the decisions made.

Overall, the smart drone workflow is designed to enable the drone to operate autonomously and perform tasks efficiently.
